UPCOMING CAPABILITIES

Engineering milestones currently undergoing laboratory prototyping and architectural bench testing. Spanning non-invasive clinical biometrics, affective companion ergonomics, next-gen silicon accelerators, and multimodal ambient intelligence.

● PHASE 2.0 // ECOSYSTEM

Google Health Integration

Seamless bidirectional synchronization between FocusBot's daily attention telemetry and the Google Health Connect framework.

  • ✓Correlates sleep recovery metrics with next-day study focus retention
  • ✓Logs active desk duration, ergonomic posture scores, and fatigue intervals
  • ✓Unified health telemetry accessible across Android, WearOS, and web
API: GOOGLE HEALTH CONNECT ACTIVE R&D
⚡ PHASE 2.5 // SENSORY

Contactless Heart Rate & Blood Pressure

Remote Photoplethysmography (rPPG) optical pulse detection through micro-vascular blood volume changes on facial skin.

  • ✓Measures real-time Heart Rate Variability (HRV) with ±2 BPM accuracy
  • ✓Identifies acute sympathetic stress peaks and exam burnout markers
  • ✓Triggers non-intrusive guided breathing and heart-rate recovery prompts
OPTICAL: rPPG 30 FPS PROTOTYPING
● PHASE 2.0 // KINEMATICS

Full-Body Ergonomics Tracking

33-point 3D kinematic skeleton extraction continuously evaluating desk ergonomics and spinal health.

  • ✓Detects forward neck angle (“tech-neck”) and lumbar spine slump
  • ✓Identifies asymmetrical shoulder lean and repetitive posture strain
  • ✓Smart standing-desk height guidance and posture realignment cues
VISION: 3D SKELETAL POSE ACTIVE R&D
● PHASE 2.0 // AFFECTIVE

Guided Micro-Breaks & Calisthenics

Embodied physical exercise guidance utilizing FocusBot's 2-DOF pan-tilt articulation and emotive screen.

  • ✓Leads interactive neck stretches, shoulder rolls, and 20-20-20 eye relief
  • ✓Physical head nodding and gaze mirroring to enforce break compliance
  • ✓Dynamically schedules intervals based on cognitive fatigue score
INTERACTION: KINEMATIC MIRROR ALPHA VALIDATION
⚡ PHASE 3.0 // ARCHITECTURE

Next-Gen Neural Hardware Architecture

Upgraded dual-SoC silicon platform featuring dedicated on-device neural acceleration for sub-10ms edge vision models.

  • ✓Dedicated 1.2 TOPS Neural Processing Unit (NPU) for local inference
  • ✓Ultra-wide 120 FPS optical sensor array with low-light HDR optics
  • ✓Modular magnetic expansion bay for environmental sensory pods
SILICON: 1.2 TOPS DUAL-SOC SCHEMATICS
● PHASE 2.5 // POWER

Magnetic Inductive Charging Dock

Cordless Qi2 / MagSafe-compatible desk pad enabling autonomous power delivery and continuous desk presence.

  • ✓15W high-efficiency magnetic alignment pad with zero cable tethering
  • ✓“Zen Sleep” docking sequence with closed digital eyes & ambient breathing glow
  • ✓Integrated thermal protection with battery cycle longevity telemetry
POWER: QI2 WIRELESS / 15W LAB BENCH
⏳ PHASE 2.5 // IOT SYNC

Circadian Lighting & Smart Desk Sync

Smart home interoperability dynamically controlling ambient workspace lighting based on fatigue and daylight curves.

  • ✓Native Matter and Zigbee protocol integration for Philips Hue & smart lamps
  • ✓Transitions from 6500K daylight for alertness to 2700K amber in evening
  • ✓Blue-light reduction triggers when cognitive exhaustion is classified
PROTOCOL: MATTER / ZIGBEE 3.0 SPEC READY
⚡ PHASE 2.0 // COGNITIVE

Gemini Live Conversational Voice Tutor

Bidirectional audio streaming powered by Gemini Live API, turning FocusBot into an intelligent spoken study partner.

  • ✓Natural low-latency verbal dialogue with conversational interruption support
  • ✓Socratic study coaching: explains complex algorithms, quizzes chapters aloud
  • ✓Real-time glance tracking and nod synchronization while speaking
ENGINE: GEMINI 3.8 LIVE IN DEVELOPMENT
● PHASE 2.0 // DEFENSE

NFC “Distraction Shield” Tap Station

Physical focus gateway utilizing an embedded NFC coil to enforce distraction-free mobile study sprints.

  • ✓Resting your smartphone on FocusBot's base locks non-essential apps
  • ✓Triggers automated iOS Focus / Android Do Not Disturb mode
  • ✓Prevents impulsive phone checking through tangible physical friction
GATEWAY: NFC HARDWARE TAG PROTOTYPING
⏳ PHASE 2.5 // SENSORY

Adaptive Acoustic Noise Shield

Dynamic sound masking that listens to ambient room clamor and plays masking frequencies through the I2S speaker.

  • ✓Real-time decibel & frequency profiling via digital MEMS microphone
  • ✓Generates adaptive pink noise, binaural beats, and brownian soundscapes
  • ✓Masks sudden café, library, or dorm speech bursts without headphones
AUDIO: 20HZ - 20KHZ MASKING BENCH TESTING
