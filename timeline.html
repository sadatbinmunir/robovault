WEEK 1 ENGINEERING REPORT: PROJECT SELECTION & SPECIFICATION INITIATION

During the inaugural sprint of the robotics project, the engineering unit reviewed candidate initiatives across Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) and computer vision assistive systems. Following comprehensive problem formulation, Project FocusBot was chosen as our official robotics capstone initiative.

  • [✓] Project FocusBot Chosen: Officially adopted FocusBot—an AI-powered interactive desk companion providing real-time posture tracking, study guidance, and multimodal encouragement.
  • [✓] Visual Form Factor & Identity: Conceptualized a spherical desk companion with 2-DOF pan-tilt articulation, expressive digital eye display, and ambient status illumination.
  • [✓] Dual-Tier Pipeline Architecture: Established hardware/software boundaries decoupling low-latency 30 FPS edge computer vision (ESP32-S3 + camera) from high-level reasoning via Google Gemini 2.0 API.
  • [✓] Specification Dossier: Outlined physical dimensions (12cm x 10cm), color palette, 6 key facial screen expressions, and operational flow from distraction alerts to break reminders.

WEEK 2 ENGINEERING REPORT: PROJECT FINALISATION & HARDWARE SOURCING

Project finalised and parts selection done, considering further improvement. Following team synthesis and advisor alignment, the research squad established hardware component intercompatibility, confirmed bill-of-materials procurement, and mapped out edge model optimizations.

  • [✓] Project Finalised: Consolidated project specifications, operational states (Study, Distraction Alert, Break Reminder, Sleep), and behavioral interaction scripts into a finalized architecture document.
  • [✓] Parts Selection Done: Confirmed core hardware components—ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 dual-core MCU, OV5640 5MP camera module, SSD1306 128x64 OLED screen, dual SG90 micro-servos for pan-tilt kinematics, and WS2812B NeoPixel aura ring.
  • [✓] Considering Further Improvement: Investigating whisper-quiet stepper/FOC actuators to minimize servo buzzing during deep focus sessions, evaluating dedicated LDO voltage regulation to suppress PWM transient noise, and planning on-device model quantization.
  • [✓] Team Alignment Session: Conducted a collaborative planning review to assign module ownership across embedded firmware, edge computer vision, circuit schematic capture, and cloud synapse integration.
